Attendance Management System

A Digital Solution for Student Attendance

1. Project Background

The Attendance Management System is a proposed software application designed to make student attendance recording easier, faster and more reliable.

Traditional attendance methods can require lecturers to manually record students' attendance. This can take time and may result in errors. Our system aims to provide a digital method for recording attendance.

2. Problem Statement

Manual attendance systems can be time-consuming and may allow inaccurate attendance records. Lecturers also need an efficient way to monitor attendance during lectures.

Our proposed system allows students to record their attendance digitally by scanning a temporary QR code generated by the lecturer.

3. Main Features

  • Student registration and login.
  • Lecturer registration and login.
  • Lecturer-generated dynamic QR codes.
  • Students scan the QR code to record attendance.
  • GPS/location verification.
  • QR code expiration for improved security.
  • Lecturer attendance dashboard.
  • Student attendance records.
  • Secure database for storing attendance information.

4. How the System Works

  1. The lecturer logs into the system.
  2. The lecturer creates a temporary QR code for the lecture.
  3. The QR code is displayed in the lecture venue.
  4. Students log into the application.
  5. Students scan the QR code.
  6. The system checks the student's location.
  7. The system verifies the QR code.
  8. The student's attendance is recorded.
  9. The lecturer can view the attendance records in real time.

5. Benefits of the System

  • Reduces manual attendance recording.
  • Saves lecture time.
  • Improves attendance record accuracy.
  • Makes attendance information easier to access.
  • Provides lecturers with real-time attendance information.
  • Improves security through temporary QR codes and location verification.
